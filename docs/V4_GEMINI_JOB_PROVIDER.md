## Native capabilities and continuity
- Actual Gemini webSearchQueries and grounding chunks are retained; missing required grounding remains incomplete, not a successful search. Redirect landing pages are independently read and registered as leads, never accepted as verified jobs solely from a citation.
- Optional GEMINI_STREAM=1 enables native SSE, with fallback only on explicit transport rejection. GEMINI_URL_CONTEXT=1 enables native URL Context for public-only JD-source recovery, not resume payloads. Both remain off until the synthetic operator probe reports native support.
- Gemini discovery defaults to adaptive parallel research unless explicitly disabled. Workers connection scheduling limits are per request, not total AI-call limits. A below-target Gemini completion gets an independent review before final partial delivery; exact repeated no-progress work is still stopped transparently rather than billed indefinitely.
- Independent background execution requires a private Workflow Worker and Pages service binding. Do not claim it is active until deployed, bound, enabled and tested with cancellation/continuation.

Context memory now rotates candidate, evidence and unresolved pages automatically for Gemini, grows accepted pages when more records exist, and reduces page size on actual provider context rejection. Original records remain intact. Discovery records measured wave latency, and report batches adapt after partial errors. Failed verification batches preserve completed evidence and retry from the original offset. Cloudflare connection headroom and evidence-validation rules remain intentional; no claim of unlimited provider capacity.

Active session slots and opaque Workflow tickets now refresh on actual authorized execution, under the same session-slot lease as cancel/close. Abandoned records retain their ordinary expiry; signed unmanaged pointers remain short-lived. Retrying a verification phase caches independently verified JD reads by candidate revision, skips already evidence-scored completed jobs after retryable scoring failures, and retains unresolved leads. Oversized public reads automatically yield byte windows with explicit incomplete status, not fabricated full JDs. The synthetic probe additionally inspects model metadata and countTokens without uploading resumes or creating persistent remote caches; provider support and privacy/retention must be reviewed before explicit caching.
